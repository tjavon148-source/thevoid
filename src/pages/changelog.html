Changelog.

What changed, in plain words.

Now on the Chrome Web Store

November 2026

  • The Void installs from the Chrome Web Store and updates itself. No more unzipping or Developer mode.
  • Your personal key is shown on the page you land on right after you pay.

0.8.5 latest

November 2026

  • Vault redesigned in The Void's black and red, with the starfield.
  • Vault categories that group folders and collapse, plus a larger sidebar.
  • Vault templates: setup playbook, pre-market plan, post-session review, and a pinned "My rules" card.
  • Notes and trades have Screenshots and Videos tabs. Vault videos are included in the Vault backup.
  • Trades are grouped by week, and day cards show chart thumbnails.
  • New app icons.

0.1.0 first release

November 2026

  • Journal overlay for TradingView.
  • Tradovate Orders CSV import, with fills paired into trades.
  • Dashboard: month calendar, equity curve, win rate, profit factor.
  • Trade review: setup tags, notes, R multiple, recap videos.
  • Weekly reviews, pre-trade checklist and the session clock.
  • Share cards, multiple accounts and backups to a folder.
  • PIN-locked Vault for private notes.

Known limits: Tradovate is the only import format so far.
