Thanks for buying The Void. Three steps and you are set up.

Getting your key...

1. Your key for The Void

Paste this into The Void the first time you open it. Keep it private. Screenshot it or save it somewhere safe in case you need it again.

2. Add it to Chrome

Add The Void to Chrome

Click Add to Chrome on the store page and confirm. Then pin The Void, open TradingView, and paste your key from step 1 when it asks. The setup guide walks through the rest. Lost your key? Press Get my key in the Discord downloads channel.

3. Join the Discord for support and updates

Join the Discord

Accept the agreement, then type /claim in the get-access channel and paste this purchase code. It gives you the Buyer role, which unlocks support and the buyers chat. You can do this any time; The Void works without it.

The code works once, for one Discord account.

Something looks wrong? Write to support@gethevoid.com with the email you paid with.
