Set it up.

About five minutes. You need Chrome on a desktop or laptop, your key and the install button (both on the page you land on right after you pay), and your Tradovate Orders CSV.

1. Get your key

Right after you pay, the page you land on shows your key. Copy the whole key, which starts with VOID1.. It is personal to you, so keep it private. Lost it? Write to support@gethevoid.com with the email you paid with. If you have joined the Discord, you can also press Get my key in downloads.

2. Install from the Chrome Web Store

On the same page, press Add The Void to Chrome. It opens the Chrome Web Store. Click Add to Chrome, then confirm. Chrome keeps it up to date for you.

3. Pin the extension

Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin The Void so it is one click away.

4. Open TradingView

Go to TradingView and open a chart. The Void appears over the chart and asks for your key the first time. Paste it and press Activate. It is saved on this computer and checked on this computer, so you only do this once.

5. Import your orders

In Tradovate, export your Orders as a CSV. In The Void, open Import and drop the file in. Entries and exits are paired into trades, and your dashboard fills in.

6. First settings

  • Time zone, so your session clock and day boundaries are right.
  • Fee per contract, so net results match your statements.
  • Risk limits, which feed the session clock's daily limits.

Before you update

Back up first. Open Settings and use Export backup to save a copy to your computer. Your journal lives in your own browser, and a backup is the safe way to move or restore it.

Stuck? Write to support@gethevoid.com, or ask in the Support channel if you have joined the Discord (optional).
