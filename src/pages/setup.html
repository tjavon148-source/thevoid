Set it up.

About five minutes. You need Chrome, the zip from the Buyer channel in the Discord, and your Tradovate Orders CSV.

Walkthrough, about 1:42. The video was made before the key step was added: get your key in the Discord first, then paste it when The Void asks on the chart. The install steps are illustrations, and all numbers shown are sample data.

1. Get your key

In the Discord, open get-your-key and press Get my key. Only you can see the reply. Copy the whole key, which starts with VOID1.. It is personal to your Discord account, so keep it to yourself.

2. Unzip the download

Save the zip from the Buyer channel, then unzip it to a folder you will keep, such as Documents. Chrome reads the extension from that folder every time it starts, so do not delete or move it later.

3. Open Chrome's extensions page

Type chrome://extensions into the address bar and press Enter.

4. Turn on Developer mode

Use the switch in the top right of the extensions page. The Void is installed from a folder, which is why Chrome needs this on.

5. Load unpacked

Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder, the one that contains manifest.json. The Void appears in your list.

Chrome may show a message about developer mode extensions each time it starts. That is normal for extensions installed this way. You can dismiss it.

6. Pin the extension

Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin The Void so it is one click away.

7. Open TradingView

Go to TradingView and open a chart. The Void appears over the chart and asks for your key the first time. Paste it and press Activate. It is saved on this computer and checked on this computer, so you only do this once.

8. Import your orders

In Tradovate, export your Orders as a CSV. In The Void, open Import and drop the file in. Entries and exits are paired into trades, and your dashboard fills in.

9. First settings

  • Time zone, so your session clock and day boundaries are right.
  • Fee per contract, so net results match your statements.
  • Risk limits, which feed the Cockpit's daily limits.

Before you update

Back up first. Open Settings and use Export backup to save a copy to your computer. Your journal lives in your own browser, and a backup is the safe way to move or restore it.
