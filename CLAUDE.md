# The Void website (read this first)

You are building the marketing site for **The Void**, a paid trading-journal Chrome extension. Read BRIEF.md fully before writing code. The owner is not a developer and relies on your judgment: make decisions, explain them in one or two lines, and keep every deploy working.

Standing rules
- The owner has Claude Pro and wants every capability used when it gives a better or faster result (subagents, scheduled tasks, browser or screenshot checks). Raise opportunities instead of waiting to be asked.
- Never type or request passwords, card or bank details. Do not publish anything that is not on the approved copy list without flagging it.
- Never ship the extension's source code on the site. The demo is a purpose-built replica with fake data.
- No income, profit or results claims. All numbers on the site are sample data and labelled so.
- No invented reviews, user counts or logos. Social proof only when real quotes exist.
- Commit in small milestones with clear messages. Keep `main` always deployable (Cloudflare auto-deploys from it).
