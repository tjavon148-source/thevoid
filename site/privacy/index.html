The extension

Your trades, notes and Vault contents are stored in your own browser. The creator does not receive them. The extension does not connect to your broker.

This website

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and does not track you across sites. There are no advertising trackers and no cookies set by this site. The live demo runs on made-up sample data and makes no network calls.

Payments and Discord

When you buy, Whop processes your payment and holds your payment details. When you join the Discord server, Discord and the server's bot handle your Discord account information, such as your username and roles. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Email

If you write to support@gethevoid.com, the message is used only to reply to you.
