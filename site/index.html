Questions.

What do I need to run it?

Chrome on a desktop or laptop, a TradingView account, and Tradovate order exports. It does not run on phones or tablets, so buy and install from your computer, or come back to the page you land on after paying.

Does it place trades or give signals?

No. It is a journal. It reads the order files you import and helps you review them. It never connects to your broker.

Which brokers does it import from?

Tradovate order exports work today. Other formats are not supported yet.

Where is my data stored?

In your own browser. You can back up to a folder on your computer.

Is it affiliated with TradingView or Tradovate?

No. The Void is an independent tool and is not made, endorsed or supported by TradingView, Tradovate or any prop firm.

Do I need Discord?

No. Your key and the install button are on the page you land on after you pay. The Discord is optional, for support, updates and the buyers chat.

What about refunds?
